New to options? Three words you'll see everywhere
Strike priceThe price you're locked into buying or selling at, no matter where the stock actually goes.
PremiumWhat you pay (or get paid) for the contract — your cost if you buy it, your income if you sell it.
BreakevenThe stock price where the trade stops losing and starts winning, once premium is accounted for.

Before any position: the six habits
Probability > max profitA high-probability small win beats a low-probability big one, run enough times.
Let IV Rank pick the strategyHigh IV Rank → sell premium. Low IV Rank → buy premium.
~45 DTE in, 21 DTE outEnter around 45 days out; manage or close by 21 regardless of P&L.
Take the 50%, skip the dramaMany close credit trades at 50% of max profit rather than riding to expiration.
Small size, high occurrenceNo single trade should be a big slice of the account.
Defined risk until you don't need to beSpreads and condors cap the downside; undefined risk wants a bigger account.
